## Krangled PoE Trade

Krangled PoE Trade is an experimental fork of
[Awakened PoE Trade](https://github.com/SnosMe/awakened-poe-trade), focused on
krangled league mechanics and other fork-specific features.

This project would not exist without Alexander Drozdov
([SnosMe](https://github.com/SnosMe)), who created Awakened PoE Trade and
continues to maintain the foundation this fork builds upon. We are deeply
grateful to Alexander and every upstream contributor for their sustained work.
