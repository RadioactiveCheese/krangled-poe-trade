The banner appears only when a saved equipped item fits the same slot. Its colour is (better rows − worse rows), an unweighted count, so in this example the ring is "better" even though it loses 17 life. Click it to expand the same tables as the compare widget. With two rings saved, tabs let you pick which ring to swap.

How it fills

  • List calls character-window/get-characters, and Import calls get-items through the app proxy. This needs a public profile or a login in the built-in browser. The account name comes from price-check settings.
  • Only the 10 gear slots are imported. Gems are added by hand: press the compare hotkey on a skill gem and click "Add as new skill", then press it on a support gem and click "Add to my supports".
  • ★ marks the main setup. Skill DPS and gear-swap impact use only that setup. "Socketed in" is just a label.
  • 🌐 marks an item imported from the site. Hover a row to show ✕ (remove).

Model (fork's buildTotals)

hit  = Σtype (weapon[type] + (gearFlat[type] + supportFlat[type]) × eff)
       × Π support "more" (scoped) × gem Attack Damage %
aps  = weaponAPS × (1 + Σ inc AS) × Π more AS
crit = weaponCrit × (1 + Σ inc global crit), multi 150 + Σ
dps  = hit × aps × (1 + crit × (multi − 100))

Left out: passive tree, auras, "% increased damage" from any source, enemy resists, accuracy, DoT, and spells. Weapon elemental damage is split evenly into fire/cold/lightning. The deltas are only useful as a relative signal for one skill.
